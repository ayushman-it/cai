5-Minute Quickstart

CuboidPilot provides a modern alternative to traditional, static chatbots. Instead of rigid decision trees that frustrate visitors, CuboidPilot employs Cai—an autonomous, semantically grounded AI Agent that acts as your 24/7 first-line support specialist and top inbound SDR.

1
Upload Documents
Provide PDF manuals, pricing sheets, and website FAQs. Cai trains in seconds.
2
Paste 1-Line Tag
Embed the async script into your website HTML, Webflow, Shopify, or WordPress.
3
Close on WhatsApp
When visitors leave desktop, they continue in WhatsApp with full session context intact.
Key Principle: Strict Grounding
Cai will never hallucinate or make up discounts or contractual claims. If an answer cannot be verified within your approved documentation, Cai gracefully captures the prospect's details and initiates a warm handoff to your human counselors.

Step 1: Setting Up Your Business Profile

The onboarding begins at onboarding/business.html. Here you configure your organization's legal name, primary website URL, industry sector, and time zone.

Required Parameters
Business Name Appears in Cai's greeting and official chat headers.
Website URL Domain whitelisted for CORS and script widget embedding.
Industry Selects pre-tuned semantic prompts (SaaS, Education, Clinics, Real Estate, etc.).
Operating Timezone Calibrates human counselor business hours vs. 24/7 AI coverage.
Configuration Tip
Selecting the correct industry automatically configures Cai's default conversational tone. For example, selecting Healthcare & Clinics enables respectful, HIPAA-compliant patient intake prompts, while SaaS & B2B prioritizes technical validation and enterprise SDR qualification.

Step 2: Defining Strategic Goals & Workflow

At onboarding/goals.html, you establish the primary mission for CuboidPilot. Your choice dictates how aggressively Cai engages visitors and when it initiates lead capture forms:

Support & Deflection

Prioritizes answering repetitive Tier-1 questions from docs. Intervenes only when visitors explicitly ask for human assistance.

Inbound Sales & SDR

Discovers prospect team size, use case, budget, and booking intent during natural conversation before routing to sales.

WhatsApp Continuity

Converts desktop drop-offs into persistent WhatsApp threads, allowing counselors to follow up without email spam.

Hybrid Autonomous Desk

Combines 24/7 instant FAQ resolution with intelligent commercial opportunity detection and Closing Radar alerts.

Step 3: Cai Knowledge & Document Grounding

Located at onboarding/ai.html, this is the foundational core of your AI Agent. Cai's responses are 100% determined by what you upload here.

Supported Knowledge Sources:

  • PDF Documents: Catalogues, course brochures, SLA policies, terms of service, technical specifications.
  • Website URLs & Sitemaps: Enter your help center root (e.g. https://acme.com/help) to crawl and index public pages automatically.
  • Structured Q&A Pairs: Explicit questions with exact approved answers for critical policies (e.g., refund policies or pricing exceptions).
Grounding Best Practices
Ensure your pricing sheets contain clear currency designations (e.g. USD $ or INR ₹) and seat tiers. If pricing is customized per client, specify: "Custom enterprise pricing requires speaking with sales" so Cai directs prospects to your contact form.

Grounding Strictness Threshold

You can toggle Cai's confidence boundary between Strict (0.85 semantic score—will only respond if directly in the text) and Balanced (0.75 semantic score—synthesizes answers across multiple related paragraphs).

Step 4: Website Widget Integration

Onboarding screen onboarding/website.html provides your unique embed tag. Copy and paste this single script snippet into your website before the closing </body> tag:

HTML / WordPress / Shopify / Webflow 
<!-- CuboidPilot Cai AI Agent Widget -->
<script 
  src="https://cai.cuboidsoft.in/widget.js?v=5.6" 
  data-company="cp_live_cuboidsoft" 
  data-color="#111111" 
  data-position="right"
  async>
</script>

CMS Installation Quick Links:

WordPress Paste into Theme Settings → Custom Code → Footer or via WPCode plugin.
Shopify Go to Online Store → Themes → Edit Code → theme.liquid before </body>.
Webflow Go to Project Settings → Custom Code → Footer Code and publish site.

Once pasted, the onboarding wizard pings your URL in real-time. As soon as a handshake is recorded, the status indicator turns Active ping detected.

Step 5: WhatsApp Cloud API & Mobile Continuity

At onboarding/whatsapp.html, you connect your official WhatsApp Business Account. This powers 1-Tap WhatsApp Continuity.

Configuration Fields
Phone Number ID

Retrieved from Meta for Developers → WhatsApp → API Setup.

WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID)

Your official business profile container in Meta Business Manager.

Permanent System User Token

Bearer token generated under Business Settings → System Users with whatsapp_business_messaging permissions.

Notification & Triage Preferences

CuboidPilot protects your counselors from notification fatigue. You can configure:

  • High-Intent Qualified Leads Only: Alerts counselors when budget and enterprise requirements are confirmed.
  • Explicit Human Takeover: Alerts when the visitor clicks "Speak with a person" or expresses frustration.
  • Cart Abandonment / Pricing Inquiries: Sends alert if a visitor stays on pricing for > 45 seconds without checking out.

The final onboarding step at onboarding/ready.html runs an automated system health check:

WhatsApp Cloud Webhook Verified for Instant Counselor Alerts

Clicking "Launch Business Desk" completes your onboarding and takes you directly into your live console at app/overview.html.

Managing Knowledge Documents & Ongoing Grounding

As your business evolves, you can keep Cai up to date through app/knowledge.html:

  • Instant Document Refresh: Upload new PDF policies or add promotional pricing sheets. Indexing completes in under 30 seconds.
  • Crawl On Demand: Hit Re-crawl URLs to pull fresh documentation from your blog or help docs.
  • Unresolved Inquiries Audit: The Gaps Radar shows customer inquiries that Cai was not confident enough to answer, letting you write targeted Q&A pairs with a single click.

Real-Time Live Chat Takeover

Inside app/conversations.html, counselors can monitor visitor sessions in real time:

1. Autonomous Observation: While Cai is answering, messages appear with a subtle green AI badge.

2. 1-Click Takeover Button: Pressing Take Over Conversation immediately pauses Cai for that session, alerting the visitor: "Counselor [Name] joined the conversation".

3. AI Copilot Assist: When typing as a human counselor, Cai suggests grounded reply drafts based on your verified knowledge base to speed up resolution.

Closing Radar & Commercial Intent Scoring

The app/closing-radar.html module automatically categorizes active visitors into 3 commercial intent tiers:

Cold (Tier 3) General browsing, basic FAQ queries. Cai answers autonomously with zero human intervention.
Warm (Tier 2) Comparing plans, asking about integrations, visited pricing page > 2 times.
Qualified (Tier 1) Explicit team size provided, enterprise SLA requested, calendar booking initiated.

Client JavaScript SDK Reference

The CuboidPilot widget exposes a lightweight global JavaScript API on window.Cai for programmatically triggering chats, passing user attributes, or binding to custom buttons on your site:

JavaScript SDK Methods 
// Open the Cai chat widget
window.Cai && window.Cai.open();

// Close the Cai chat widget
window.Cai && window.Cai.close();

// Pass authenticated user context from your app
window.Cai && window.Cai.identify({
  userId: "user_98124",
  name: "Arjun Mehta",
  email: "arjun@example.com",
  plan: "Enterprise",
  company: "Nexora Systems"
});

// Trigger a contextual prompt automatically
window.Cai && window.Cai.prompt("Tell me about the Enterprise SLA options");

Integrates with Meta's official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Supports bi-directional incoming inquiries, template notifications, interactive buttons, and media messages.

Callback URL: https://cai.cuboidsoft.in/api/whatsapp_webhook.php
Required Scopes: whatsapp_business_messaging, whatsapp_business_management
Instagram Direct Messages (Graph API) Social CRM

Automatically qualifies prospects who DM your business or reply to stories. Syncs inquiries into the unified Cai inbox in real time.

Callback URL: https://cai.cuboidsoft.in/api/instagram_webhook.php
Webhook Fields: messages, messaging_postbacks
Google Calendar & Meet Auto Booking

Performs real-time free/busy conflict checking against counselor calendars and automatically creates Google Meet links upon meeting booking.

Redirect URI: https://cai.cuboidsoft.in/api/google_callback.php
Scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events
Transactional Email (SMTP) Instant Alert

Sends immediate email notifications to sales reps upon high-intent qualification, and dispatches conversation summaries to clients via standard SMTP.

Supported Ports: 587 (TLS), 465 (SSL), 25
Compatible: SendGrid, Postmark, AWS SES, Google Workspace

Google Sheets Apps Script Receiver (Ready-to-Paste)

In your Google Sheet, open Extensions > Apps Script, paste this code, and click Deploy > New deployment > Web app (Access: Anyone):

Google Apps Script (Code.gs) 
function doPost(e) {
  try {
    var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getActiveSheet();
    var payload = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
    var data = payload.data || {};

    // Auto-create column headers if sheet is blank
    if (sheet.getLastRow() === 0) {
      sheet.appendRow(["Timestamp", "Lead Name", "Phone", "Email", "Intent Score", "Summary", "Source"]);
      sheet.getRange(1, 1, 1, 7).setFontWeight("bold").setBackground("#f3f4f6");
    }

    // Append lead row
    sheet.appendRow([
      new Date().toISOString(),
      data.name || "Anonymous Visitor",
      data.phone || "",
      data.email || "",
      data.intent_score || "Tier 3",
      data.summary || "",
      payload.source || "Website Widget"
    ]);

    return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ status: "success", row: sheet.getLastRow() }))
      .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
  } catch (err) {
    return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({ status: "error", message: err.toString() }))
      .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
  }
}

Universal Outbound Webhooks & Events Specification

Configure custom HTTP POST webhooks in app/channels.html to stream real-time events to your proprietary backend, AWS Lambda, Zapier, or Make. Every webhook request includes an X-Cuboid-Signature header generated via HMAC-SHA256 for cryptographic authentication.

POST https://api.yourdomain.com/cai/webhook
Content-Type: application/json
User-Agent: CuboidPilot-Webhook-Agent/2.0
X-Cuboid-Event: lead.qualified
X-Cuboid-Signature: sha256=d5b4a9f3... (Hex digest)
X-Cuboid-Timestamp: 1768924000

Verifying Webhook Signatures (Node.js & PHP)

Node.js (Express) 
const crypto = require('crypto');

function verifySignature(req, signingSecret) {
  const signature = req.headers['x-cuboid-signature'];
  const expected = 'sha256=' + crypto
    .createHmac('sha256', signingSecret)
    .update(req.rawBody)
    .digest('hex');

  return crypto.timingSafeEqual(
    Buffer.from(signature || ''),
    Buffer.from(expected)
  );
}
PHP 
$secret = 'whsec_your_signing_secret';
$rawPayload = file_get_contents('php://input');
$signature = $_SERVER['HTTP_X_CUBOID_SIGNATURE'] ?? '';

$expected = 'sha256=' . hash_hmac('sha256', $rawPayload, $secret);

if (!hash_equals($expected, $signature)) {
    http_response_code(401);
    die('Signature verification failed');
}
