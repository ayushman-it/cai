Traditional chatbots rely on rigid decision trees, static buttons, and frequently get stuck on unforeseen questions ("Sorry, I didn't understand that"). Cai is an autonomous AI Customer Agent built on advanced semantic grounding. It understands natural-language business inquiries, reasons across your verified knowledge documents, and resolves routine questions instantly while intelligently looping in human specialists the moment high-intent deals or complex issues arise.

Cai operates under a Strict Grounding Protocol. It only draws answers from approved business documents you configure (PDFs, pricing spreadsheets, service catalogs, and live URLs). If a customer asks a question outside your approved corpus, Cai does not fabricate facts—it intelligently flags the conversation for Human Action and prepares a concise summary for your team.

When a website visitor prefers to continue on mobile, CuboidPilot generates a continuous session token via the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. The entire chat context—including previous questions, answers, and lead details—transfers to WhatsApp instantly without requiring the customer to re-authenticate or repeat their inquiry.

Human counselors or sales agents can step into the active thread on Web or WhatsApp with a single click, equipped with an AI-generated briefing summarizing the customer's intent and questions.

Deployment takes under 5 minutes. Simply embed our lightweight script tag (< 12KB) into your website's header. It works out-of-the-box with WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Next.js, and custom HTML. Once added, point Cai to your website URL or upload your docs, and it begins answering customer questions immediately.
