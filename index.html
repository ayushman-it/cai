Traditional chatbots rely on rigid decision trees, static buttons, and frequently get stuck on unforeseen questions ("Sorry, I didn't understand that"). Cai is an autonomous AI Agent built on advanced semantic grounding. It understands complex, natural-language business inquiries, reasons across your verified knowledge documents, and resolves 50%+ of questions in under 0.8 seconds without requiring human staff.

Cai operates under a Strict Grounding Protocol. It only draws answers from approved business documents you upload (PDFs, pricing spreadsheets, service catalogs, and live URLs). If a customer asks a question outside your approved corpus, Cai never fabricates facts—it proactively captures the visitor's contact info and offers to connect them with your human counselor.

When a website visitor prefers to continue on mobile, CuboidPilot generates a continuous session token via the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. The entire chat context—including previous questions, answers, and lead details—transfers to WhatsApp instantly without requiring the customer to re-authenticate or repeat their inquiry.
